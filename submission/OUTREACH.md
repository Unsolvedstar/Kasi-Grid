# Outreach messages (to send yourselves)

These are drafts for the conversations only you can have. Edit them, and only state things that are true.

## 1. A home-care or medical equipment supplier, pharmacy or clinic
Goal: real prices, or a message saying they would partner. This turns the Care tab from "placeholder" into "validated".

> Hi [NAME]. We are two 22-year-olds (a software developer and a biomedical engineering student at Wits) entering the FNB App of the Year Hackathon with Kasi Grid, an app that helps neighbours look out for each other during power and water outages. One part lets a family that depends on a concentrator or other medical device see how long their battery lasts and book a battery backup, equipment or a carer.
> Could we ask three quick things? (1) Roughly what do you charge for a 5L oxygen concentrator rental, a 14L cylinder, and a battery backup? (2) How do you handle prescriptions for oxygen? (3) Would you be open to being the equipment partner if we pilot this? We are not asking for anything now, only whether this is something you would consider.

What to bring back: price list or quotes, prescription process, and a yes, maybe or no on partnering.

## 2. A bank contact (FNB or another)
Goal: one conversation about whether an outage savings pot or stock cover makes sense.

> Hi [NAME]. We are entering the FNB App of the Year Hackathon (climate resilience and essential services track) with Kasi Grid, an app that brings a community closer together when the lights go out. It shows verified, community-level outages and what an outage costs a small shop in lost sales and stock. We think there is a product for a bank here, for example a small outage savings pot, stock insurance, or finance for backup power. Could we have 15 minutes to ask whether that is something a bank would consider, and what it would need to see from us?

Do not claim any FNB product exists or that FNB has agreed to anything.

## 3. A spaza owner
> Hi [NAME]. When the power goes off, roughly how many hours does it last and what does it cost you in stock or sales? We built a free app where neighbours verify outages together, and it shows that cost live and what would fix it. Could I show you in 5 minutes and get your honest reaction?

## 4. A community group admin or ward contact
> Hi [NAME]. We built a free app where neighbours report power and water outages and others confirm them, so people know what is real, and neighbours can offer each other help, such as phone charging or a lift to the clinic. Could we share it in the [GROUP] WhatsApp group for one week as a trial? We will report back what we find.

## 5. A native-speaker review (isiZulu, Sesotho, Afrikaans)
> Hi [NAME]. Could you read the [LANGUAGE] text in our app and tell us what is wrong or unnatural? It is about 400 short lines in `src/locales.js`, and I can send you a simple list. Even 20 minutes on the Today tab would help.

## After each conversation
Write down: who, when, what they said, and what they agreed to. Put the one or two strongest quotes on the traction or partners slide, with their permission.
